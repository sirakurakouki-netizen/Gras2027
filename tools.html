About This Site

This site is a collection of open source games, gathered in one place purely for fun. Everything here is meant for casual, personal enjoyment on your own time — playing with friends, killing time at home, or just exploring what open source game developers have built and shared with the world.

These games are not meant to be used in school. This site is not designed for, intended for, or endorsed for use during class, on school networks, or on school-managed devices. School time is for learning — play these at home. Any use of this site inside a school is not intended and is solely the user's own choice and responsibility.
